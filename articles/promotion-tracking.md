# Promotion Tracking (Belts & Stripes)

From your **Profile** page, use **Add promotion** to log a new belt or stripe, with the date awarded. Your current rank is shown as a belt bar with a black rank-tip (red for black belt) and up to 4 stripe marks, and syncs automatically across the app (profile header, promotion history carousel).

The promotion history carousel shows your full timeline oldest-to-newest, with how long you held each rank.

## Correcting a mistake
Tap a past promotion entry and choose **Edit** or **Delete**. There's no undo for deletion, so double-check the date before confirming.
