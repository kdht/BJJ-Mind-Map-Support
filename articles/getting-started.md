# Getting Started

BJJ Mind Map helps you organize your Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu game as a visual map of positions, transitions, and submissions, alongside a training journal and an AI Coach.

## 1. Create an account
Sign up with email, Google, or Facebook from the app. A free **Drill** tier is available with no credit card required.

## 2. Create your first mind map
Tap **+ New Map** on the Mind Maps tab. Start from a blank canvas or a starter template (e.g. "Closed Guard Basics").

## 3. Log your training in the Journal
The Journal tab gives you a rich-text notebook per topic. Switch to raw Markdown mode any time from the toolbar if you prefer writing in plain text.

## 4. Ask the AI Coach
Tap the floating Coach charm from any screen to ask for a technique breakdown, a game plan, or to have a mind map built for you conversationally.

## 5. Track promotions
Log every stripe and belt promotion from your Profile — the timeline syncs automatically to your current rank shown across the app.
