# Creating Your First Mind Map

1. Go to the **Mind Maps** tab and tap **+ New Map**.
2. Choose a blank canvas, or start from a template.
3. Add nodes for positions (blue), submissions (red), sweeps (green), and takedowns (purple) — drag to connect related nodes with arrows.
4. Double-tap a node to add notes, embed a video link, or link it to a journal entry.
5. Use the color legend at the top to keep your map readable as it grows.
6. Share a map with a training partner from the map's menu (••• → Share).

Tip: start small with a single position's most common follow-ups rather than trying to map your entire game at once — maps grow naturally as you add more nodes over time.
