# Account & Billing

## Tiers
- **Drill** — free, core mind maps + journal + promotion tracking.
- **Roll** — paid, adds more mind maps, collaboration, and journal notebooks.
- **Coach** — paid, adds the AI Coach and the full feature set.

## Managing your subscription
- **Mobile (iOS/Android):** subscriptions are billed through the App Store or Google Play and renew automatically until cancelled in your store account settings — BJJ Mind Map cannot cancel or refund a store subscription directly.
- **Web:** subscriptions started via the website are billed directly; manage or cancel anytime from Profile → Subscription.

## Deleting your account
Profile → Settings → **Delete account**. This permanently removes your mind maps, journal entries, and promotion history and cannot be undone.

## Dormant accounts
Per our [Terms of Service](https://bjjmindmap.com/terms.html), free accounts inactive for 12+ months may be deactivated after a 30-day advance notice email. Logging in at any time resets the clock.
