# Troubleshooting Sign-In

## "Invalid credentials" with email/password
Double-check caps lock, and use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen to reset it.

## Google or Facebook sign-in doesn't respond
- Make sure the app has an active internet connection.
- Try signing out of and back into your Google/Facebook account on your device, then retry.
- Update the app to the latest version from the App Store / Google Play.

## Stuck on a loading screen after sign-in
Force-close and reopen the app. If it persists, [submit a bug report](https://vnext.bjjmindmap.com/submit.html) with your device model and app version (Profile → About).

## I deleted my account by mistake
Account deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no recovery. [Contact support](https://vnext.bjjmindmap.com) if you believe this happened in error.
