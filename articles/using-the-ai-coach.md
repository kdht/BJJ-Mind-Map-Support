# Using the AI Coach

The AI Coach is available from the floating charm button on every screen (bottom-right).

## What it can do
- Explain or break down a specific technique or position.
- Build a game plan for an upcoming roll or competition.
- Create or edit a mind map for you conversationally — just describe what you want mapped.
- Answer general BJJ questions, in text or by voice.

## Important safety note
AI Coach output is generated automatically and is **informational only** — it is not professional coaching or medical advice, and is not a substitute for in-person instruction by a qualified instructor. BJJ carries an inherent risk of injury; always train under qualified supervision and within your own limits. See the full disclaimer in the app's [Terms of Service](https://bjjmindmap.com/terms.html).
